# Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Title
Project Legal – AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

## Overview
Project Legal is an AI-powered legal document generator designed to simplify the process of creating legal documents.

## Problem Identified
Creating legal documents manually can be time-consuming and requires knowledge of legal formats and terminology.

## Proposed Idea
The project proposes an AI-powered system that generates structured legal documents based on the user's requirements and inputs.

## Objectives
- To simplify legal document preparation.
- To reduce the time required for document drafting.
- To provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
- To generate structured legal documents using AI.

## Expected Outcome
A user-friendly AI-based system that helps users generate legal documents efficiently and systematically.
